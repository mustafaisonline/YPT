Problem Statement

The Global Reach Gap

Why exceptional Data & AI companies remain invisible to the world

Most Data & AI companies don't lack capability.

They lack access to a global customer base.

Hundreds of talented startups, niche vendors, and specialized consultancies build world-class solutions, yet remain confined to their local markets. Without a global stage, their innovations never reach the decision-makers who need them most.

Traditional marketing channels are prohibitively expensive, analyst reports favor incumbents, and cold outreach rarely breaks through. The result? A massive gap between what the market needs and what it can see.

Sky-High Marketing Costs

Traditional B2B marketing in Data & AI is designed for enterprises with deep pockets:

  • •Conference sponsorships cost $20K–$100K+
  • •Analyst inclusion requires expensive pay-to-play models
  • •Digital ad campaigns burn budget with low conversion

For most companies, these channels are simply out of reach, leaving them with no scalable way to build awareness.

Geographic Isolation

Brilliant solutions built in one region never cross borders:

  • •No network or relationships in target markets
  • •Cultural and language barriers limit outreach
  • •Local success doesn't translate to global credibility

Without a global platform, even the best products stay trapped in their home market.

No Access to Decision-Makers

Reaching the right people is the hardest part:

  • •Cold outreach has <2% response rates in B2B tech
  • •Decision-makers trust peer recommendations over ads
  • •Building credibility from scratch takes years

LTAD bridges this gap, giving you direct, authentic access to a global network of Data & AI leaders.
