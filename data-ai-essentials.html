Why Data & AI Literacy Matters

Organizations across every industry are investing heavily in Data and Artificial Intelligence.

However, many professionals struggle because they do not understand the fundamental concepts behind modern data ecosystems and AI technologies.

This training bridges that gap by providing a practical understanding of how data and AI work together to support business decisions, innovation, and digital transformation.

Participants will gain the confidence to engage with Data Engineers, Data Scientists, Architects, and AI teams using a common language and shared understanding — the core outcome of effective Data Awareness Training and AI Training Malaysia programs.
