Why Organizations Need an Agentic AI Strategy

Many organizations rushed into Generative AI initiatives without fully understanding business value, governance requirements, operational challenges, and production realities.

As Agentic AI emerges, many organizations risk repeating the same mistakes.

Organizations need to answer important questions:

  • • What is Agentic AI?
  • • How is it different from Generative AI?
  • • Which business processes should be automated?
  • • What risks must be managed?
  • • What governance is required?
  • • How do we move from experimentation to production?

This training provides a practical roadmap for those questions — pair with the Value Discovery Canvas™ to prioritize initiatives and Agentic AI Solutions when you are ready to execute.

Course Structure

1

The Evolution of AI

Rule-Based Systems, Machine Learning, Generative AI, and Agentic AI — understanding how AI evolved toward autonomous systems.

2

The Reality of AI Adoption

Lessons from previous AI initiatives, why projects fail, common adoption challenges, and managing expectations.

3

What is Agentic AI?

Fundamentals, goal-oriented systems, autonomous decision making, tool usage, memory, planning, and reflection.

4

Agent Types and Agent Ecosystems

Core agents, supporting agents, multi-agent systems, and emerging agent patterns.

5

Components of Agentic AI Systems

Planning, memory, tool integration, execution, monitoring, and feedback loops.

6

Risks, Governance & Responsible Adoption

Governance, compliance, security, privacy, human oversight, trust, and accountability.

7

Business Use Cases

Employee onboarding, meeting management, policy discovery, knowledge management, project tracking, and helpdesk automation.

8

The PVP Approach

Productionizable Viable Product (PVP) — why many AI solutions fail to reach production and how to improve adoption success.

9

Enterprise Case Studies

ReliaQuest GreyMatter, Deloitte Zora AI, and enterprise adoption scenarios.

10

Building an Agentic AI Roadmap

Opportunity identification, prioritization, governance, adoption planning, and scaling responsibly.
