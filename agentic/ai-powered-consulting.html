FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is AI Powered Consulting for?
Startups, SMEs, founders, and business leaders worldwide who need websites, apps, chatbots, portals, or automation — without enterprise budgets or months-long discovery. If you are scaling into full data platform work, see Data & AI Services.
How much does a project cost?
Every project is scoped individually. AI-powered delivery reduces cost versus traditional development. Pricing depends on complexity and scope. Contact us for a tailored quote.
How long does delivery take?
Many MVPs and prototypes ship in days or weeks. Landing pages and initial proof-of-concepts can be ready within 24 hours. Production-ready solutions typically take 1–3 months depending on scope.
Can you enhance existing systems?
Yes. We modernize, extend, or integrate with your current tools — CRMs, spreadsheets, legacy apps — and recommend the most cost-effective path without unnecessary rebuilds.
Do you provide support after launch?
Yes. Maintenance, enhancements, and advisory are available as your solution grows. Teams wanting in-house capability can upskill via AI-Powered Product Development training.
Can you integrate with existing software?
Yes — CRMs, ERPs, payment gateways, cloud services, messaging platforms, and custom APIs.
Do I need a complete set of requirements before starting?
No. We help business owners refine requirements through discovery and rapid prototypes — ideal when you have a problem or idea but not a full technical specification.
