# Reception assets

All 3D models and material textures in this folder come from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), published under the CC0 licence (public domain, no credit required, commercial use allowed). Textures were downscaled for the web.

Models: sofa_02, modern_arm_chair_01, mid_century_lounge_chair, Ottoman_01, coffee_table_round_01, side_table_tall_01, modern_wooden_cabinet, modern_ceiling_lamp_01, calathea_orbifolia_01, potted_plant_02, potted_plant_04, ceramic_vase_01, brass_vase_01, standing_picture_frame_01.
Materials: marble_01, plastered_wall_04, walnut_veneer.

The reception desk, wall panels, display screens, paintings and logo wall are built in code for this site.

## Interior floors, walls and fabrics (Poly Haven, CC0, resized to 512 px)
terrazzo_tiles, diagonal_parquet, brushed_concrete_03, white_stucco, ash_veneer, fabric_leather_01, rough_linen, granite_tile_03 (https://polyhaven.com)
anthurium_botany_01 (plant, real model replacing the simple plants on the floors)

## Exterior (assets/hww/outdoor)

All textures are CC0 and were downscaled and re-compressed for the web (the two leaf images were also merged with their alpha masks and resized).

Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), CC0:
- Textures: leafy_grass (lawn), precast_stone_paving (plaza and apron), concrete_pavers (paths and the perimeter walk), concrete_slab_wall (slab edges, kerbs, planters, sign wall), bark_brown_01 and bark_brown_02 (tree trunks and branches).
- Leaf images, taken from the textures of the models island_tree_02 (broadleaf tree, also used for hedges and shrubs) and jacaranda_tree (feathery fronds). Only the leaf diffuse and alpha maps are used; none of the (very heavy) tree meshes are.
- Re-used from the reception set: walnut_veneer (bench slats).

ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), CC0:
- Metal009 (brushed steel for the lift, frames, mullions and rails).

The facade glazing, lift, roof, sign lettering, trees, hedges, planters, benches and street lamps are built in code for this site from the textures above.
