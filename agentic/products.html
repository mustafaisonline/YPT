Online Wholesale & Retail Management System

Sell wholesale and retail goods online, and run the orders behind them

Ask for a demo →

One system to sell to the public, to shopkeepers and to wholesalers, and to run everything behind the sale: prices, stock, payments, delivery and reports.

Buyers browse a catalogue, fill a cart and check out as a guest or with an account. Each buyer sees the price that belongs to them, pays the way they prefer, and follows the order to the door. Behind the shop, staff process orders, check payments, keep stock and prices right, and see how the business is doing, with every change recorded.

The problems it takes off your desk

  • Orders live in chat and callsEvery order is captured once, with its items, payment and delivery, and visible to the buyer and the staff.
  • Three kinds of buyer, one price listRetail, trade and wholesale prices, minimum quantities and quantity breaks, applied automatically for each buyer.
  • Prices and stock change oftenChange a price or a stock count in one place and the shop updates everywhere.
  • Payment is informalCash on delivery, mobile wallet or bank transfer, with proof uploaded by the buyer and verified by a person.
  • One product, many pack sizesEvery pack size is its own item with its own price and stock, grouped under one product page.

Invoice Management System

Invoices handled, not chased

Ask for a demo →

Create invoices, record the money that comes in, and always know who owes what, with every change on record.

The product keeps the ledger of a small business: customers, products and services, invoices with numbers that never skip, and the receipts that settle them. An administrator creates invoices and records payments; an assistant can read everything and download invoice PDFs. Totals are calculated on the server, statuses move by themselves as money arrives, the team is told when an invoice is issued, and every change is written to an audit trail.

The problems it takes off your desk

  • Invoice numbers and totals done by handNumbers are assigned in order with no gaps, and totals are calculated on the server, so they always add up.
  • Who has paid and who has notRecord a payment once, split it across invoices if needed, and each invoice moves to partly paid or paid by itself.
  • The oldest debts get forgottenEvery customer shows what is current and what is 1-30, 31-60, 61-90 and over 90 days late, plus a running statement.
  • The team finds out lateWhen an invoice is issued, the team gets a pop-up inside the app and an email with the invoice PDF attached.
  • No trail when something changesEvery create, edit, issue, delete, payment and setting change is recorded with who, when, and the values before and after.

Who uses it

Administrator

Creates and issues invoices, records and reverses payments, manages customers, items, users, settings, imports, exports and the audit trail.

Assistant

Read-only: sees invoices, customers and payments and downloads invoice PDFs. Gets notified when an invoice is issued.

Invoices

  • Create and edit invoicesPick a customer, add lines, set one discount and one tax rate. The server recalculates every amount and never trusts the screen.
  • Numbers that never skipEach invoice gets the next number from its series, with your own prefix, pattern and yearly or never-ending count.
  • Products and services listAdd an item once with its price. Invoice lines suggest it and fill the price.
  • Payment terms and due datesThe due date follows the customer's payment term, such as net 14 days.
  • Six currenciesPKR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED and MYR, chosen per invoice. Totals are kept separately for each currency.
  • Duplicate an invoiceStart a new invoice from an old one.
  • Edit after issue, safelyThe number stays, the version goes up, and two people cannot overwrite each other's changes.
  • Customer details frozen on the invoiceName, address and tax number are kept as they were on the day it was issued.
  • Invoice PDFA branded PDF with your bank details and terms, downloadable from the invoice.

Get paid

  • Record received amountsCash, bank, cheque, card or other, with a date, reference and notes.
  • Split one payment across invoicesThe amounts allocated must add up to the amount received, and paying more than is owed is refused.
  • Status moves by itselfIssued becomes partly paid, then paid, as receipts are recorded or reversed.
  • Reverse a receiptUndo a payment with a reason, and the invoices go back to what they were.
  • Receipt numbersEvery receipt has its own number in its own series.

Customers

  • Customer recordsContacts, billing and shipping addresses, payment term and currency, with a customer number suggested for you.
  • AgeingOpen invoices sorted into current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90 and over 90 days late, for every currency.
  • StatementEach invoice and receipt in order with a running balance, ready to read out on a call.
  • Deactivate, never loseA customer can be switched off and on again, so the history stays.
  • Overdue at a glanceAn Overdue filter on the invoice list shows what is late.

Tell the team

  • Instant pop-upWhen an invoice is issued, the other people in the business see a notification right away.
  • Email with the PDFEveryone who should know gets an email with the invoice PDF attached, sent from a queue that retries if the mail server is busy.
  • Choose who is toldSwitch notifications on or off per person, and add extra addresses such as a shared inbox.
  • Notification inboxA bell with unread notifications, mark one or all as read, and a list of emails waiting or failed.

Control and trust

  • Two roles, fourteen permissionsAdministrator and assistant, checked on the server for every page and every action.
  • Audit trailAppend-only record of who did what and when, with the values before and after, filterable by person, type and date.
  • Only an administrator can deleteDeleting an invoice is permanent, but a full copy is kept in the audit trail.
  • Safer sign-inAccounts lock for 15 minutes after five wrong passwords, and sessions can be listed and signed out from another device.
