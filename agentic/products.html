Product 01 · Built with Jun, Builder

Online Wholesale & Retail Management System

Sell wholesale and retail goods online, and run the orders behind them

Ask for a demo →

One system to sell to the public, to shopkeepers and to wholesalers, and to run everything behind the sale: prices, stock, payments, delivery and reports.

Buyers browse a catalogue, fill a cart and check out as a guest or with an account. Each buyer sees the price that belongs to them, pays the way they prefer, and follows the order to the door. Behind the shop, staff process orders, check payments, keep stock and prices right, and see how the business is doing, with every change recorded.

The problems it takes off your desk

  • Orders live in chat and callsEvery order is captured once, with its items, payment and delivery, and visible to the buyer and the staff.
  • Three kinds of buyer, one price listRetail, trade and wholesale prices, minimum quantities and quantity breaks, applied automatically for each buyer.
  • Prices and stock change oftenChange a price or a stock count in one place and the shop updates everywhere.
  • Payment is informalCash on delivery, mobile wallet or bank transfer, with proof uploaded by the buyer and verified by a person.
  • One product, many pack sizesEvery pack size is its own item with its own price and stock, grouped under one product page.

Who uses it

Public shopper

Browses and buys at retail prices, with or without an account.

Shopkeeper

Applies for a trade account and, once approved, buys at trade prices.

Wholesaler

Approved for wholesale prices and bigger quantity breaks.

Order desk

Staff who process orders, verify payments and keep stock right.

Administrator

Everything: catalogue, prices, customers, approvals, reports and settings.

36 features, grouped by what they do for the buyer and for the owner. Open the Modules tab to see them by screen.

Shop and discover

  • Catalogue by categoryProducts are grouped so buyers find what they want in a few clicks.
  • Pack sizes as separate itemsEach size has its own price and stock, under one product page.
  • Search, filters and sortingFilter by price range, pack size, stock and offers, and sort the results.
  • Rich product pagesPhotos, description, shelf life and badges such as organic, new and featured.
  • Offer pricesA struck-through "was" price shows buyers what they save.

Buy

  • Cart that stays honestPrice, minimum quantity and stock are checked again when the order is placed.
  • Guest checkoutBuyers can order without creating an account.
  • Delivery address, zone and time slotBuyers choose where and roughly when; the fee follows the zone.
  • WhatsApp orderingA chat button for buyers who prefer to ask first.

Pricing and trade

  • Three price tiersRetail, trade and wholesale prices on every pack size.
  • Minimum order quantitiesSet per tier, so trade and wholesale orders start at a sensible size.
  • Quantity breaksOrder more and the unit price drops automatically.
  • Trade accounts by approvalBuyers apply with business details; trade and wholesale prices show only after approval.
  • Price locked on the orderThe price a buyer saw is stored on the order line and never changes afterwards.

Pay

  • Cash on delivery, mobile wallet or bank transferPayment instructions are shown up front, with the account details.
  • Proof of paymentBuyers enter a transaction ID and can upload a screenshot or slip.
  • Verified by a personStaff verify or reject each payment, and a rejection carries a reason.
  • Confirm only when paidA prepaid order is confirmed only after its payment is verified; cash orders are marked paid on delivery.

Deliver and track

  • Delivery zonesEach zone has a fee, a free-delivery threshold and an estimated delivery time.
  • Order timelinePlaced, confirmed, packed, out for delivery, delivered, with who changed it and when.
  • Tracking for guestsAnyone can follow an order with the order number and phone number.
  • Cancel while still openBuyers can cancel an order that is only placed; the stock goes back on the shelf.

Run the business

  • Overview dashboardToday's orders, sales, what needs attention, and the charts that matter.
  • Stock per pack sizeStock counts and reorder levels, with a low-stock list.
  • Edit products and pricesNames and descriptions in two languages, prices per tier, show or hide a product.
  • Customers and approvalsBuyer list, change a buyer type, and approve or reject trade applications.
  • Enquiries inboxContact and franchise enquiries with a status, so none are forgotten.
  • Banners and payment detailsShow or hide home-page banners and keep payment account details up to date.

Control and insight

  • Eleven reportsSales by day, product, category and buyer type, payments, stock and more, each as a CSV download.
  • Audit trailEvery admin change is recorded: who, what, when, and the old and new value.
  • Sign-in log and web trafficSee who signed in, and how visitors use the shop by page, device and language.
  • Data dictionary and database viewA readable map of the data and a read-only view of the tables.
  • Roles and permissionsOrder desk staff and administrators see different screens, checked on the server.
  • No oversellingStock is taken the moment an order is placed, so the same item is never sold twice.

Everywhere

  • English and UrduThe whole shop and admin switch language, including right-to-left layout.
  • Works on a phoneDesigned for small screens first, and installable as an app.
