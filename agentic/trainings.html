FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who buys YPT corporate training programs?
HR leaders, L&D teams, talent development managers, and capability leads procuring workforce upskilling in Data and AI. Programs also suit university partners and government agencies building national or organizational digital capability.
Who should attend these training programs?
Audiences vary by program — business users and managers (Data & AI Essentials), practitioners (Data Blueprint), architects (Enterprise Data Architecture), leaders (Agentic AI Strategy), builders (AI-Powered Product Development), and professionals seeking career guidance (Career Mentorship). Each program page lists ideal participants for L&D planning.
Are the courses beginner-friendly?
Yes. Data & AI Essentials suits beginners with no technical background. Advanced programs assume foundational data knowledge. We help HR and L&D teams map participants to the right starting point via our learning pathway.
Can training be customized?
Yes — YPT specializes in corporate Data and AI training tailored to your industry, technology environment, and team skill levels. Content, case studies, and exercises are customized for government, financial services, telecom, healthcare, universities, and enterprises.
Is virtual delivery available?
Yes. All programs support virtual instructor-led, onsite, and hybrid formats for teams across Malaysia and internationally — ideal for distributed workforces and regional L&D rollouts.
Do participants receive certificates?
Yes. Certificates of completion are issued for all programs upon successful completion — useful for HR records, CPD tracking, and internal capability frameworks.
Can YPT deliver onsite training?
Yes. Onsite delivery at your premises across Malaysia and internationally — suited to private team cohorts, executive workshops, and graduate development programs.
Are corporate training packages available?
Yes — executive awareness, team workshops, capability development, graduate programs, and private bootcamps. Request a training proposal. Pair programs with the Value Discovery Canvas™ to align learning investments to business priorities.
