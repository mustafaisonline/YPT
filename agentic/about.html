FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who founded Your Partner Technologies?
YPT was founded by Usama Jameel, a seasoned Data & AI strategist with extensive experience leading enterprise-scale transformations across Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and beyond. The team includes senior advisors with deep domain expertise.
What is YPT's philosophy on vendor neutrality?
We maintain zero vendor affiliations, no reseller agreements, and no referral incentives. This ensures every recommendation, from data platform selection to AI framework choices, is based purely on what delivers the best outcome for your organization.
Does YPT work with international clients?
Yes. While headquartered in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, we serve clients across the Asia-Pacific region and the Middle East. We deliver engagements both on-site and remotely, adapting our engagement model to fit your location and requirements.
What makes YPT's team different?
Our team members are practitioners, not just advisors. Every strategist on our team has hands-on experience building and scaling data platforms, AI solutions, and governance frameworks in real enterprise environments. See our work in action on the case studies page.
Does YPT work with startups or only enterprises?
While our core focus is enterprise advisory, we also work with growth-stage companies building their data foundations. Our Value Discovery Canvas and training programs are particularly valuable for organizations at any stage.
How does YPT ensure quality in its engagements?
We follow a structured methodology that combines strategic frameworks with hands-on execution. Every engagement includes clear deliverables, milestone reviews, and knowledge transfer to ensure lasting impact beyond our involvement.
