FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does it mean that YPT is a distributor or reseller?
YPT is authorized to sell and support specific partner products listed on this page. We help organizations evaluate, procure, implement, and maintain these solutions — combining vendor technology with independent advisory and delivery capability.
Is YPT biased toward partner products?
No. YPT remains vendor-neutral in advisory engagements. We only recommend partner products when they genuinely fit your requirements. Our LTAD 2.0 evaluations cover the broader market, including non-partner tools.
Can YPT implement partner products for us?
Yes. Beyond licensing, YPT provides architecture, deployment, integration, training, and ongoing support through our Executive Services and AI Powered Consulting teams.
How do I list my product on the YPT website?
Product companies interested in distribution or reseller partnerships should contact us with product details, target markets, and partnership goals. We review fit against our client base and advisory portfolio.
Are pricing and licensing available on product pages?
Pricing varies by scope, region, and deployment model. Product pages describe capabilities and use cases. Contact YPT for a tailored quote and licensing discussion.
