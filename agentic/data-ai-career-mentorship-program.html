FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this training?
No. This is not a training course. Training teaches a fixed curriculum to a group. Mentorship is personalized one-on-one guidance focused on your career strategy, decisions, and growth.
Is this coaching?
It includes coaching elements — accountability, mindset, and career decisions — but it is grounded in deep Data & AI industry expertise. Your mentor is a practicing architect and consultant, not a generic life coach.
Is this consulting?
Not in the traditional sense. Consulting delivers solutions for organizations. Mentorship develops you — your skills, positioning, roadmap, and career trajectory.
Who is this suitable for?
Aspiring and experienced professionals in Data, Analytics, Engineering, Architecture, AI, and Leadership — from graduates to future CDOs and independent consultants.
How many sessions will I receive?
It depends on the package you choose. The Career Assessment is a single 90–120 minute session. Professional Mentorship includes monthly sessions over 3 months. Executive Mentorship runs over 6 months with priority session access. See Training Investment for regional pricing.

Is the roadmap personalized?
Yes — entirely. Your roadmap is built from your background, goals, strengths, gaps, and target role. No two mentorship journeys are the same.
Can I combine mentorship with YPT training?
Absolutely. Many mentees pair mentorship with YPT programs such as Data & AI Essentials, The Data Blueprint, or Enterprise Data Architecture. Your mentor recommends training only when it serves your roadmap.

Yes. AI transition is one of the most common mentorship paths — whether you are a software engineer, data engineer, analyst, or leader moving into AI and Agentic AI roles.
