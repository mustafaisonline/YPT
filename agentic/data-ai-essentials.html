Why Data & AI Literacy Matters

Organizations across every industry are investing heavily in Data and Artificial Intelligence.

However, many professionals struggle because they do not understand the fundamental concepts behind modern data ecosystems and AI technologies.

This training bridges that gap by providing a practical understanding of how data and AI work together to support business decisions, innovation, and digital transformation.

Participants gain confidence to engage with data engineers, scientists, architects, and AI teams using shared language — the starting point before advancing to The Data Blueprint or leadership programs like Agentic AI Strategy & Adoption.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is Data & AI Essentials designed for?
Business users, managers, students, graduates, analysts, product owners, HR professionals, and operations teams who need data and AI literacy — not hands-on engineering. HR and L&D teams often book this as an organization-wide foundation before specialist tracks on the training catalog.
Do I need technical experience?
No. Designed for non-technical professionals — no programming, coding, or prior data background required. Ideal first step before practitioner programs like The Data Blueprint.
Is this course suitable for beginners?
Yes. Foundation-level and beginner-friendly — one of the most accessible data literacy programs for individuals and corporate teams in Malaysia.
Is coding required?
No. Focus is conceptual understanding, business context, and awareness of Generative AI and Agentic AI — not development or tooling.
Can organizations request private sessions?
Yes. Private corporate cohorts tailored to your industry and team size. Contact us or see corporate training options.
Is virtual delivery available?
Yes — virtual instructor-led, onsite, and hybrid delivery across Malaysia and internationally.
Do participants receive certificates?
Yes. Certificate of Participation upon completion — suitable for HR records and internal capability tracking.
