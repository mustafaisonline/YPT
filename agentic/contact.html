FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How can I contact Your Partner Technologies?
You can reach us via WhatsApp at +60 11-2320 9594, through our contact form on this page, or by email. We typically respond within 24 hours on business days.
What happens during a discovery call?
A discovery call is a free 30-minute conversation where we understand your current challenges and goals. We then recommend the right path, whether that is enterprise advisory, execution, AI Powered Consulting for a website or app, training, or a combination.
Does YPT offer free consultations?
Yes, we offer a complimentary discovery call to understand your needs and determine if there is a fit. This no-obligation conversation helps both parties assess alignment before any formal engagement.
How quickly can YPT start an engagement?
For advisory and workshop engagements like the Value Discovery Canvas, we can typically begin within 1-2 weeks. Larger project-based engagements require a scoping phase which usually takes 1-2 weeks before execution begins.
Can I reach YPT outside business hours?
You can send us a WhatsApp message or fill out our contact form any time. We monitor messages regularly and will respond as soon as possible, typically within 24 hours.
Where is YPT located?
YPT is based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. We serve clients across the Asia-Pacific region and the Middle East, delivering engagements both on-site and remotely. Learn more about us.
