FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is vibe coding?
Vibe coding is AI-assisted product development — using prompts, modern AI tools (e.g. Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT), and structured workflows like PromptOS to build working applications without writing code from scratch. This flagship program teaches the full product lifecycle, not just prompting.
What is PromptOS?
PromptOS is YPT's AI product development operating system — frameworks, prompt libraries, templates, and accelerators included with enrollment. You keep using it long after training ends to build faster.
Who is this flagship program for?
Entrepreneurs, startup founders, product managers, citizen developers, freelancers, and corporate innovation teams who want to build real apps and MVPs — not traditional software engineers. For executive strategy, see Agentic AI Strategy & Adoption.
Do I need programming experience?
No. Helpful but not mandatory. Product thinking, prompts, prototyping, and deployment — designed for builders and innovators, not CS graduates.
Is coding required?
No traditional coding. AI-assisted workflows and modern tools handle implementation. Focus is validate → design → build → test → deploy.
What is included with enrollment?
Training plus PromptOS Starter Edition, Data Blueprint Foundations module, templates, prompt libraries, capstone project assets, and certificate. Full data practitioner track → The Data Blueprint.
Bootcamp vs Accelerator vs Mastery — which should I choose?
Bootcamp (2 days) — fastest, most immersive. Accelerator (2 weeks, 2 hrs/day) — best for working professionals. Mastery (4 weeks, 1 hr/day) — best for beginners and universities. Same curriculum and certification in all formats.
Which AI tools are covered?
Modern AI development concepts with examples that may include ChatGPT, Cursor, Claude, Windsurf, and emerging platforms. Emphasis on workflows and principles, not vendor certification.
Is the course hands-on?
Yes — rapid prototyping exercises and a capstone project from idea to working solution. You leave with a portfolio-ready project.
Can the training be customized for teams?
Yes — startup teams, innovation labs, and corporate builder cohorts. Contact us or see corporate training.
Is virtual delivery available?
Yes — virtual instructor-led, onsite, and hybrid across Malaysia and internationally.
Are certificates provided?
Yes — Certificate of Participation upon completing this flagship AI-powered product development program.
