FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services does Your Partner Technologies offer?
YPT offers a segmented portfolio: AI Powered Consulting for SMEs and startups; Data & AI Services for delivery leaders and data teams; Agentic AI for innovation and transformation teams; Value Discovery Canvas™ for initiative shaping; LTAD and LTAD 2.0 for community insight and tool evaluation; corporate training for HR and L&D; and Strategic Advisory for executives.
How does YPT's engagement model work?
Engagement models are matched to the buyer: advisory retainers for leadership, project-based delivery for data and AI teams, Value Discovery Canvas workshops for initiative sponsors, pilots and PoCs for innovation teams, and corporate training programs for HR and L&D — each tailored to organizational maturity.
Can YPT handle end-to-end project delivery?
Yes. For delivery leaders and data teams, YPT provides hands-on execution across data engineering, analytics, AI development, and platform modernization — from discovery through deployment and knowledge transfer.
How does YPT integrate AI into existing data infrastructure?
Delivery and architecture teams work with YPT to assess the current landscape, identify integration points, and design pragmatic solutions — whether adding Agentic AI workflows, ML pipelines, or modernizing the data platform. Technology choices remain vendor-neutral.
Does YPT offer proof-of-concept engagements?
Yes. Innovation and delivery teams frequently start with focused proof-of-concept or pilot engagements — particularly for AI, ML, and Agentic AI — to validate value before committing to larger programs.
How does YPT ensure knowledge transfer?
Knowledge transfer is built into every engagement through documentation, corporate training, and hands-on mentoring — so HR, L&D, and internal teams can sustain outcomes without ongoing dependency.
