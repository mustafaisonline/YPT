About

With over 23 years of experience in the Data & AI industry, Mustafa Qizilbash has established himself as a thought leader and practitioner in the field. He is CDMP (Certified Data Management Professional) certified and has worked with leading organizations including PETRONAS Digital.

His work spans strategic advisory and hands-on execution across data strategy, governance, and AI adoption.
